Robot Master Color Project

This is an offshoot of a previous ranking project, wherein I aimed to rank all the classic mega man themes according to some semi-objective musical criteria. There are clear problems with such an undertaking, based mainly on the fact that there are not a lot of “bad” mega man themes. This project is more reasonably designed to measure the feel of a theme, along 3 vectors of intensity, where a low scoring “relaxed” track is a style, not a grade.

Energy, density and gravity are simply three aspects of the music that I could easily identify. “Intensity” is simply the culmination of all three—I think it works well because Mega Man is traditionally intense, and so it tends to stand out when it’s not. But more obviously, this is all really just a glorified reason to listen to Mega Man music! — metaphist

Intensity to Color

Intensity is a track's three scores added together, from 3 (all 1s) to 15 (all 5s). For intensity, low is 3–7, mid is 8–10 and high is 11–15.
