Song Color Visualizer

Every stage theme is scored for energy, density and gravity, and the three scores become one color. Pick a game or build a playlist to hear them, and see how the colors spread across the series.

Why Color

This is an offshoot of Rob Moura’s Spectreview color rating system.

Energy, density and gravity are simply three aspects of the music that I could easily identify for classic Mega Man. “Intensity” is simply the culmination of all three—I think it works well because Mega Man is traditionally intense, and so it tends to stand out when it’s not. But more obviously, this is all really just a glorified reason to listen to Mega Man music! — metaphist

The Visualizer
